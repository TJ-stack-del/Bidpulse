# BidPulse: Procurement Intelligence & Proposal Acceleration

## Product Overview
BidPulse is an end-to-end procurement intelligence platform for small-to-medium government contractors (GovCon). It orchestrates a 6-stage RFP lifecycle from discovery to submission execution.

## Core Personas
- **Client/Contractor:** Business owners and proposal leads managing bids.
- **Platform Admin:** Reviewers managing the fulfillment and audit pipeline.

## The 6-Stage Pilot Lifecycle
1. **Discovery & Intake Drafting:** Solicitation ingestion and PIA attestation.
2. **Compliance Review:** Dynamic checklist for FAR clauses and technical thresholds.
3. **Assembly & Drafting:** Artifact generation (Capability Statements, etc.).
4. **Quality & Admin Audit:** Internal sign-off and fulfillment.
5. **Client Review:** Final inspection and feedback logging.
6. **Submission Execution:** Sealing, logging, and archival.

## Key Features
- **Matched Opportunities:** AI-driven bid shortlisting.
- **Compliance Matrices:** Interactive checklists.
- **Immutable Audit Trail:** Legal integrity and pilot logging.
- **Legal Safeguards:** Integrated TOS, MNDA, and FAR attestations.
