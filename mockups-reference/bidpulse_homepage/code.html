Questions contractors actually ask

Will you guarantee I win the bid?

No — and we'll never tell you otherwise. We put together a complete, accurate submission on your behalf. The agency makes the award decision, not us.

What if I don't have a certification a bid requires?

We flag it before you spend more time on that bid, not after. If a solicitation requires a set-aside certification you don't hold, we tell you plainly so you can decide whether it's worth pursuing.

How fast is turnaround?

We aim to have your paperwork ready within 48 hours of you sending us a bid. If something's running long, you'll hear from us — you shouldn't have to chase a status update.

Can I look before I pay?

Yes. Every deliverable is free to preview in full. You only pay once you're ready to download the real files.

What if you don't know something about my business?

We'll never invent a certification, an insurance number, or a past project on your behalf. If we don't have a fact from you, we mark it clearly so you know exactly what to fill in before you submit.

Which trades do you work with?

We're built especially for HVAC, janitorial, landscaping, and IT/computer-support contractors in the Jacksonville area. If you're in a different trade, reach out — we may still be able to help.
